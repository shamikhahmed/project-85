PROJECT 85 privacy

PROJECT 85 is a personal, local-first fitness app. Health data you authorize stays on your device in IndexedDB. It is not sold or uploaded to PROJECT 85 servers.

Health data

With your permission, the Android app reads steps, weight, sleep, energy, heart, and related metrics from Health Connect so your Dashboard can show honest progress. Manual entries always win over synced values. Optional write-back of weight is off unless you enable it.
