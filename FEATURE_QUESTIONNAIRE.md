## Context for the AI

I am building **PROJECT 85**, a private birthday transformation app (not SaaS).

- Start weight: **96.5 kg** (editable) → Target: **85 kg** by **15 January 2027**
- Start date: **27 October 2026** (~80 days — do not invent a 90-day runway)
- Daily defaults: 10k steps, 2200 kcal, 160 g protein, 3000 ml water, 0 cigarettes
- Stack: Vite + React + TypeScript PWA, IndexedDB only, no login, no cloud sync
- Philosophy: Apple-calm UI, honest logging, 7-day weight trends (not scale obsession), recovery not punishment, Pakistani/desi food library
- Already built: Dashboard briefing, daily log (weight/food/steps/water/workout/sleep/smoking/notes), progress charts, discipline score, Nightly Truth Check, history calendar, settings backup/export, installable PWA

## Questions (answer honestly)

### A. Lifestyle & environment
1. Where do I live / travel most weeks (city, climate, office vs WFH)?
2. Typical workday shape (desk hours, commute, prayer/meals timing)?
3. Who cooks — me, family, restaurant, office cafeteria?
4. How often do I eat Pakistani/desi meals vs Western?
5. Do I drink alcohol? Soft drinks? Chai frequency?
6. Smoking: quit attempt, taper, or just track honestly?

### B. Training & body
7. Access to gym / swimming / home weights?
8. Preferred training: strength, swim, walking, mix?
9. Injuries or pain to respect?
10. Do I care more about scale, waist, photos, or clothes fit?
11. Willing to take optional progress photos (stored only on device)?

### C. Tracking personality
12. Do daily weigh-ins help me or stress me?
13. Ideal logging time: morning, after meals, night only?
14. How many taps max for a common meal before I quit?
15. Do I want reminders? If yes, which (weigh-in, protein, steps, evening review)?
16. Prefer Focus Mode (3 priorities) or full dashboard?

### D. Accountability style
17. Soft coach, firm coach, or silent tracker?
18. OK with streaks if missed days don’t erase history?
19. Want weekly review every Sunday?
20. Any “personal rewards” after honest weeks (non-food)?

### E. Integrations (future)
21. Must-have later: Apple Health steps, Google Fit, Fitbit, none?
22. Need multi-device sync eventually, or one phone is enough?
23. Comfortable with optional encrypted cloud backup later?

### F. UX polish wishlist
24. Light mode daily, dark only, or system?
25. Urdu / bilingual labels useful?
26. Larger text / accessibility needs?
27. Anything in the current app that feels confusing, cold, or slow?

## Ask ChatGPT to output

1. Top **10 feature ideas** ranked by impact vs effort for *my* answers  
2. Which ideas **conflict** with safety (starvation, punishment exercise, shame)  
3. A **2-week build backlog** Cursor can implement next  
4. Exact acceptance criteria for the top 3 features  
5. Copy suggestions for friendlier empty states / evening review
